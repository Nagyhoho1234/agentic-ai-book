## 1. University Overview

The University of Debrecen (Debreceni Egyetem, DE) is one of Hungary's largest and most prestigious universities, with roots dating back to 1538 (the Reformed College of Debrecen). It operates **14 faculties** across **7 campuses** with over 150,000 square meters of floor space. The university has approximately **1,500 lecturers** and a total student body of approximately **30,000 national students** plus **~7,800 international students** from **130+ countries** (2025/2026 academic year).

The current Rector is **Prof. Dr. Zoltan Szilvassi**, who has stated that informatics "permeates all sciences and developments at the university," with no field uninfluenced by the discipline.

## 2. "Modern Artificial Intelligence" Elective Course

### Overview

In February 2025, the Faculty of Informatics launched a university-wide elective course titled **"Korszeru mesterseges intelligencia" (Modern Artificial Intelligence)**, a first-of-its-kind initiative at DE.

### Course Details

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Duration** | 13 weeks |
| **Credits** | 3 credits |
| **Format** | Fully online |
| **Prerequisites** | None -- no prior IT knowledge or programming skills required |
| **Eligibility** | Students from **any faculty**, plus university lecturers and administrative staff |
| **Registration** | Via NEPTUN Electronic Academic System |

### Curriculum Topics

The course provides a comprehensive overview spanning multiple fields:

- **Everyday AI applications**: smartphone assistants, chatbots, self-driving cars
- **Machine learning fundamentals**: supervised and unsupervised learning
- **Large language models (LLMs)**
- **Creative content generation**: images, music, video
- **AI in personalized medicine**
- **Ethics of AI**
- **Robotics**
- **Cloud technologies**
- **Data and cybersecurity**
- **AI in agriculture**
- **AI in arts and creative industries**
- **AI in economics**

### Leadership and Statements

The initiative was spearheaded by **Prof. Dr. Andras Hajdu**, Dean of the Faculty of Informatics. Key quotes from Dean Hajdu:

> "We aim to focus on processes where AI plays a key role."

> The course offers "deeper and broader knowledge than just simple use of AI tools."

Guest lecturers from various fields contribute to the interdisciplinary nature of the course.

### Enrollment

Specific enrollment numbers were not publicly available at the time of research, but the course is open to the entire university community (30,000+ students, 1,500+ lecturers, and administrative staff).

### Related Initiative

The **Gyorgy Hajos Data Science Collegium** also offers an AI workshop for interested participants.

## 3. AI Expert Postgraduate Diploma

### Program Overview

The Faculty of Informatics offers a **1-year (2 semester) postgraduate specialization programme in Artificial Intelligence ("AI Expert")** in English, in a hybrid format. This is described as **the only such training programme in Hungary**.

### Curriculum (14 Subject Areas, 532 Total Hours)

| Module | Type |
|--------|------|
| 1. Machine Learning | Core |
| 2. Data Protection and Cryptography | Core |
| 3. Generative Methods | Core |
| 4. Parallel Computing | Core |
| 5. AI Programming | Core |
| 6. Autonomous Vehicles | Core |
| 7. Efficient Data Visualization | Core |
| 8. Cloud Computing | Core |
| 9. Reinforcement Learning | Core |
| 10. Image Processing (Neural Networks) | Core |
| 11. Natural Language Processing and Text Mining | Core |
| 12. AI Frameworks | Core |
| 13. Big Data Handling Techniques | Core |
| 14. Thesis Work | Capstone |

**Breakdown**: 196 hours lectures + 336 hours laboratory/exercise

### Tuition and Fees

- **Tuition**: $8,000 USD per semester ($16,000 total)
- **Application fee**: $150 USD
- Includes cloud credits for lab work and exam opportunities for industry certifications

### Admission Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or Computer Science Engineering; **OR**
- Master's degree in a related IT field (for those without a CS bachelor's)

### Format and Schedule

- **Language**: English
- **Format**: Hybrid (online + face-to-face; exams require in-person attendance)
- **Next start**: February 2026 (pending minimum enrollment)
- **Application deadline**: January 15, 2026

### Industry Certifications (Optional)

- **NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute** certifications
- **Microsoft Azure** certifications:
  - Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)
  - Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900)
  - Azure AI Solution
  - Azure AI Engineer Associate

### Industry Partners

The programme was developed with support from:
- **Bosch**
- **EPAM Systems**
- **General Electric (GE HealthCare)**
- **Microsoft**
- **NI (National Instruments)**
- **NVIDIA**

### Advanced Pathway

Graduates can apply completed coursework toward the **2-year Data Science MSc**, potentially reducing that programme by up to one year.

## 6. Automotive and AI Coordination Institute

### Establishment

In **September 2025**, the University of Debrecen Senate approved the creation of the **Jarmuipari es Mesterseges Intelligencia Koordinacios Intezet** (Automotive Industry and Artificial Intelligence Coordination Institute).

### Leadership

The institute is led by **Prof. Laszlo Palkovics**, former Hungarian Minister of Innovation and Technology, who took up the position on **October 1, 2025**. Palkovics played a key role in preparations for the BMW factory in Debrecen.

### Mission

The institute's strategic goals:
- **Horizontally unite** vehicle-related, autonomous, energy supply, and AI research across the university's various faculties
- Coordinate educational unit activities, particularly **adapting education to industrial needs**
- Support scientific research in automotive and AI
- Broaden **international scientific and industrial partnerships**
- Deepen relationships with the **BMW Group Factory Debrecen** and the broader automotive supplier network

### BMW Partnership and Vehicle Laboratory

- A **4.9 billion HUF (~$13M)** vehicle research laboratory was established at the DE Vezer Street Scientific, Technological and Innovation Park
- The university received **29 billion HUF (~$79M)** in state support for automotive-related infrastructure development, including:
  - Expansion of the Faculty of Engineering
  - Renovation of the Chemistry building
  - Creation of an Automotive Laboratory

### Connection to BMW Factory

The **BMW Group Plant Debrecen** officially opened on **September 26, 2025**, representing a **EUR 2 billion investment**. The plant can produce up to **150,000 cars per year** and creates **2,000+ direct jobs**. It is the first BMW plant to rely entirely on renewable energy and produces the new BMW iX3 (Neue Klasse platform).

## 7. Komondor Supercomputer

### Overview

**Komondor** is Hungary's most powerful supercomputer, located at the **Supercomputer Center on the Kassai ut Campus** of the University of Debrecen. Named after the Hungarian sheepdog breed.

### Technical Specifications

| Specification | Detail |
|--------------|--------|
| **Type** | HPE Cray EX |
| **Peak Performance** | ~5-6 petaflops |
| **Storage** | 10 petabytes |
| **Power Consumption** | ~300 kW |
| **Cost** | 4.7 billion HUF (~$12.8 million) |
| **Impact** | Increases Hungary's HPC capacity tenfold |

### AI and Data Science Capabilities

Komondor has a **dedicated AI and big data partition** with specialized GPU computing units for targeted tasks including:
- Speech and face recognition
- Medical imaging and diagnostics
- Genetic sequence analysis
- Molecular modelling
- Material science simulations
- Climate research
- Economic/financial data processing
- AI-based intelligent applications
- Drug research and precision medicine

### Management and Access

- **Operator**: KIFU (Governmental Information-Technology Development Agency)
- **Access**: Free for higher education institutions, research institutes, and their partners; selective availability to R&D-focused companies
- **Head of Supercomputer Center**: Zoltan Gal

> "Supercomputers are needed for processing large amounts of data in almost all scientific fields." -- Zoltan Gal

### Energy Efficiency

Waste heat from Komondor is used to help heat a neighbouring municipal swimming pool.

### Timeline

- September 2022: Assembly began
- October 2022: Testing
- December 2022: Operational launch

## 11. University-Wide Digital and AI Strategy

### AI Usage Guidelines

The University of Debrecen has published official **"Iranyelvek a mesterseges intelligencia alapu rendszerek hasznalathoz a Debreceni Egyetemen"** (Guidelines for Using AI-Based Systems at the University of Debrecen).

Key principles:
- **Open approach** to AI technology, following development trends
- **Encourages** AI tool use in both educational and research processes
- Fundamental requirement: AI application must be **responsible, ethical, and transparent**
- **Faculty autonomy**: Teachers determine within faculty regulations which activities allow, support, or prohibit AI use
- **Student responsibility**: Using AI outside prescribed conditions is treated as **plagiarism**

## 14. Hungarian National AI Context

### Hungary's Artificial Intelligence Strategy

**Original Strategy (2020-2030)**: Published May 2020, developed with input from the AI Coalition and SZTAKI.

**Renewed AI Strategy (2025-2030)**: Published **September 3, 2025** by the Hungarian Government. Six main pillars:

1. **Computing capacity and infrastructure**: National AI infrastructure, Hungarian "AI Centre," national digital datasets, development tools and test environments
2. **AI competency and education**: Expanding expert base, expanding data/AI specialists, helping at-risk labour market groups, establishing an AI Innovation Centre
3. **Data and AI ecosystem**: Efficient use of public data, transparent and regulated data framework
4. **Research excellence**: Fine-tuning large Hungarian language models, EU AI Act compliance auditing, "on-premises" security-critical applications (law, healthcare), software security in generated code
5. **Applications and market development**
6. **Regulation and ethical frameworks**

### AI Coalition (Mesterseges Intelligencia Koalicio)

- Founded: **October 9, 2018**, with 78 founding members
- Current membership: **400+ members** (companies, universities, scientific workshops, government organisations)
- Initiated by **Dr. Laszlo Palkovics**, then Minister of Technology and Industry (now head of DE's Automotive and AI Coordination Institute)
- **6 working groups**: Data Industry, Applications & Market Development, International Relations, Education & Awareness, Regulation & Ethics, Technology & Security
- **AI Challenge**: Launched December 2020, aimed to introduce 1 million Hungarians to AI, with 100,000 completing free online courses
- The University of Debrecen Faculty of Informatics is a member

### Hungarian-Language AI Model Development

**PULI GPT Models** (developed by HUN-REN Hungarian Research Centre for Linguistics -- NYTK):
- **PULI GPT-3SX**: Monolingual Hungarian model trained on 32 billion words of Hungarian text
- **PULI GPTrio**: Trilingual model (Hungarian-English-Chinese), 6.7 billion parameters
- Latest models include instruction-tuned and multilingual variants

**Racka**: Efficient Hungarian LLM adaptation (4B parameter backbone, 160B token multilingual pretraining corpus)

**HuSpaCy**: Industrial-strength Hungarian NLP pipeline (tokenization, POS tagging, lemmatization, dependency parsing, NER), developed by SzegedAI team within MILAB.

**OpenHuEval**: Benchmark for testing LLMs on Hungarian-specific challenges (morphology, pragmatics, cultural knowledge, dialectal understanding).

### MILAB (National Artificial Intelligence Laboratory)

Coordinated by HUN-REN SZTAKI, MILAB promotes and finances:
- Publications, patents, technology transfer in AI
- Connection between industry, universities, and research institutes

## 16. Published AI/ML Research from Debrecen Faculty

### Medical Image Analysis (Andras Hajdu and Team)

Prof. Dr. Andras Hajdu's group is internationally recognized for **automated retinal image processing**:

- **Diabetic Retinopathy Debrecen Dataset**: Created by Antal and Hajdu, deposited at the UCI Machine Learning Repository, containing features extracted from the Messidor image set for predicting diabetic retinopathy
- **Ensemble-based diabetic retinopathy screening**: Multi-algorithm approach combining quality assessment, pre-screening, lesion-specific components (microaneurysms, exudates), and anatomical components
- **Retinal vessel segmentation**: Using directional response vector similarity and region growing (recognized as "Esteemed Paper 2015" -- Top 10-15 Papers of the Year)
- **Optic disc and macula detection**: Combining algorithms for automatic detection in fundus images
- **Retinal microaneurysm detection**: Through local rotating cross-section profile analysis
- **Review paper**: "A review on automatic analysis techniques for color fundus photographs" (PubMed: 27800125)

### GE HealthCare NLP for Hungarian Medical Texts

Joint project developing **Hungarian-language NLP algorithms** for diagnostic radiology text analysis and clinical trial patient identification (see Section 9.3).

### Agricultural Machine Learning

Faculty of Agricultural Sciences research on **maize chlorophyll estimation** using hyperspectral indices combined with ML models (SVM, Gaussian process regression, neural networks).
