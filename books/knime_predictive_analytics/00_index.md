# Predictive Analytics with KNIME -- Frank Acito (2023)

## Book Overview

**Author:** Frank Acito
**Publisher:** Springer Nature Switzerland AG, 2023
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-45630-5

This book provides a comprehensive, code-free introduction to predictive analytics using the KNIME Analytics Platform. It is designed for analysts, business professionals, and scientists who want to apply machine learning and statistical modeling techniques without writing code. KNIME's visual, drag-and-drop workflow approach makes sophisticated analytics accessible to non-programmers while still delivering production-grade results.

## Key Themes

- **No-code analytics:** Every technique is implemented purely through KNIME's visual workflow editor -- no programming required
- **End-to-end process:** Covers the full CRISP-DM lifecycle from business understanding through deployment
- **Practical datasets:** Uses real-world datasets (Cleveland heart disease, German credit, Toyota Corolla, Iris, telecom churn, US crime rates, mall customers) throughout
- **Comparative modeling:** Frequently compares multiple algorithms on the same dataset so the reader can see trade-offs
- **Cost-sensitive analysis:** Goes beyond accuracy to consider business costs of different types of misclassification
- **Reproducibility:** Every workflow is documented with node-by-node descriptions, settings, and I/O specifications

## Target Audience

Scientists, analysts, and business professionals who need to build predictive models but are not programmers. The book assumes basic statistics knowledge but no coding experience.
