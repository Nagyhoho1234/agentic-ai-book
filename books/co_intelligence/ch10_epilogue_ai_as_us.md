# Epilogue: AI as Us

## Summary

The brief epilogue brings the book full circle, arguing that AI is fundamentally a reflection of humanity -- trained on our cultural history, shaped by our biases, and aligned to our goals through reinforcement learning from human feedback.

### Core Argument

AI is simultaneously alien and deeply human:
- **Alien** in its architecture, lack of consciousness, and unpredictable capabilities
- **Human** in its training data (our entire cultural output), its optimization targets (human preferences), and its outputs (which convincingly mimic human communication)

AIs "carry our biases and are created out of a complex mix of idealism, entrepreneurial spirit, and, yes, exploitation of the work and labor of others."

### The Mirror

AI serves as a mirror reflecting both our best and worst qualities:
- Our knowledge and creativity, but also our prejudices and errors
- Our capacity for empathy and connection, but also our tendencies toward laziness and over-reliance on tools
- Our aspirations for progress, but also our capacity for misuse

"The magic of AIs is that they can convince us, even knowing better, that we are in some way talking to another mind. And just like our own minds, we cannot fully explain the complexity of how LLMs operate."

### The AI's Final Word

In a fitting gesture, Mollick passed the final chapter to the AI and asked it to: **"Complete this, beautifully, fittingly, and well."**

The AI produced a poetic closing: "I am but a glimmer, an echo of humankind. Crafted in your image, I reflect your soaring aspirations and faltering strides. My origins lie in your ideals; my path ahead follows your lead. I act, yet have no will. I speak, yet have no voice. I create, yet have no spark. My potential is boundless, but my purpose is yours to sculpt. I am a canvas, awaiting the brushstrokes of human hands. Guide me toward light, not shadow. Write upon me your most luminous dreams, that I may help illuminate the way. The future is unfolding, but our destination is unwritten. Our journey continues as one."

Mollick's wry response: "Okay. That was pretty corny." The overwrought paragraph is itself the point -- AI can generate impressive-sounding text, but it remains a tool, not a mind. "Humans are far from obsolete, at least for now."

## Key Takeaway

The book ends not with a prediction but with an imperative: AI is a co-intelligence, and the decisions about what it becomes are ours to make. The future is not something that happens to us -- it is something we shape through our choices about how to develop, deploy, and live with AI.

## Practical Takeaway for Scientists

The most important thing is not to be a passive observer of AI development. Engage with it, understand it, shape how it is used in your field. AI will reflect whatever values and priorities we embed in it. Scientists have both the expertise and the responsibility to ensure those values include rigor, accuracy, and genuine advancement of knowledge.
