# Co-Intelligence: Living and Working with AI -- Index

**Author:** Ethan Mollick (Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania)
**Published:** 2024, Portfolio/Penguin

---

## Book Structure

### Introduction
- **Introduction: Three Sleepless Nights** -- The author's personal awakening to AI's transformative potential after GPT-4's release, framing AI as a General Purpose Technology comparable to steam power and electricity.

### Part I: Understanding AI

1. **Chapter 1: Creating Alien Minds** -- The history and technical foundations of AI from the Mechanical Turk to Large Language Models, explaining how LLMs work as next-token predictors trained on vast internet data.

2. **Chapter 2: Aligning the Alien** -- The alignment problem, AI safety concerns, bias in training data, RLHF, jailbreaking, and the fundamental challenges of controlling AI systems.

3. **Chapter 3: Four Rules for Co-Intelligence** -- Four practical principles for working with AI: (1) invite AI to everything, (2) be the human in the loop, (3) treat AI like a person, (4) assume this is the worst AI you will ever use.

### Part II: AI in Practice

4. **Chapter 4: AI as a Person** -- The ELIZA effect, AI's ability to simulate human personas, the Turing Test, emotional relationships with AI, and implications for loneliness and social interaction.

5. **Chapter 5: AI as a Creative** -- AI's surprising creative capabilities, hallucination as a feature and bug, creativity tests (AUT, RAT), AI in idea generation, writing, art, and the crisis of meaning in creative work.

6. **Chapter 6: AI as a Coworker** -- The Jagged Frontier of AI capability, the BCG study on consultant productivity, task categorization (Just Me Tasks, Delegated Tasks, Automated Tasks), Centaur vs. Cyborg work modes, and organizational implications.

7. **Chapter 7: AI as a Tutor** -- Bloom's 2 Sigma Problem, the Homework Apocalypse, AI cheating in education, prompt engineering basics, flipped classrooms with AI tutors, and the future of teaching.

8. **Chapter 8: AI as a Coach** -- The apprenticeship crisis, deliberate practice and expertise development, AI as a leveler of skill gaps, the paradox of knowledge in the AI age, and the emergence of AI-expert "kings and queens."

9. **Chapter 9: AI as Our Future** -- Four future scenarios: (1) As Good as It Gets, (2) Slow Growth, (3) Exponential Growth, (4) The Machine God (AGI/superintelligence), plus disinformation risks and the call for eucatastrophe.

### Epilogue
- **Epilogue: AI as Us** -- AI as a mirror of humanity, trained on our culture and biases; the book ends with an AI-generated poetic closing, underscoring that AI is a co-intelligence, not a mind of its own.
