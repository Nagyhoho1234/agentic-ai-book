# AI for Earth and Environmental Sciences

**Authors:** Multiple contributors
**Pages:** 145-152

## Summary

This essay examines AI applications in earth and environmental sciences, including climate modelling, weather prediction, natural disaster forecasting, biodiversity monitoring, and environmental change detection. AI's ability to process large, complex, multi-dimensional datasets makes it particularly valuable in these fields.

Key applications include: improving climate model parameterisations (using ML to replace or improve sub-grid-scale physics that cannot be explicitly resolved), short-range weather prediction, detection and classification of extreme weather events, satellite image analysis for land use change and deforestation monitoring, and species identification from images or audio recordings.

The essay highlights the LEAP initiative (Learning the Earth with AI and Physics), a collaboration between the US NCAR and the NSF, which uses machine learning to improve the Community Earth System Model (CESM). This addresses one of the most important challenges in climate science: the representation of complex physical processes (clouds, convection, land surface processes) at scales too fine for explicit simulation.

## Key Policy Recommendations

- Governments should invest in AI-enhanced climate and weather prediction models as a matter of urgent public interest.
- Open data policies for environmental monitoring (satellite imagery, sensor networks) should be strengthened.
- Interdisciplinary training programmes should prepare earth scientists to use AI tools effectively.
- International cooperation on AI for environmental science should be prioritised.

## Practical Takeaways for Scientists and Institutions

- AI can dramatically improve the speed and accuracy of environmental predictions, but must be carefully validated against physical principles.
- Hybrid approaches combining physics-based models with ML are more robust than purely data-driven methods.
- Researchers should invest in standardised, well-documented environmental datasets to enable effective AI training.

## Notable Data and Statistics

- The CESM (Community Earth System Model) is a complex collection of component models simulating atmosphere, ocean, land, and ice processes.
- Climate models have traditionally struggled with sub-grid-scale processes like cloud formation; ML offers a path to improvement.
- Satellite observations generate petabytes of environmental data annually.
