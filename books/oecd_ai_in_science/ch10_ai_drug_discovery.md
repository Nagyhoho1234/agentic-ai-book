# AI in Drug Discovery and Development

**Authors:** A. Zhavoronkov and colleagues
**Pages:** 103-112

## Summary

This essay examines how AI is transforming drug discovery and development, one of the most expensive and time-consuming scientific endeavours. Traditional drug development takes an average of 10-15 years and costs over USD 2 billion per approved drug, with a failure rate exceeding 90%. AI has the potential to dramatically reduce these timescales and costs.

Key applications include: target identification (using AI to identify disease-relevant biological targets), molecular generation (using generative AI to design novel drug candidates), property prediction (predicting ADMET properties -- absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, toxicity), clinical trial optimisation (patient selection, endpoint prediction), and drug repurposing (identifying new uses for existing drugs).

The essay discusses the success of Insilico Medicine and other AI-first drug companies that have brought candidates to clinical trials in a fraction of the usual time. However, it also notes that most AI-designed drugs are still in early-stage trials and that the true test of AI's value in drug discovery will be measured by the number of AI-designed drugs that ultimately reach patients.

## Key Policy Recommendations

- Governments should support public-private partnerships for AI in drug discovery, particularly for neglected diseases where commercial incentives are weak.
- Regulatory frameworks should adapt to accommodate AI-designed drugs, including standards for validating AI-generated molecular designs.
- Open data initiatives for biological and chemical data are critical for enabling AI-driven drug discovery.

## Practical Takeaways for Scientists and Institutions

- AI can dramatically accelerate the early stages of drug discovery (target identification, lead generation) but is not yet a substitute for clinical trials.
- Wet lab validation remains essential -- AI-generated hypotheses must be tested experimentally.
- Interdisciplinary teams combining computational chemistry, biology, and AI expertise are essential for effective AI-driven drug discovery.

## Notable Data and Statistics

- Traditional drug development: 10-15 years, USD 2+ billion per approved drug, 90%+ failure rate.
- Insilico Medicine used AI to identify a novel drug target and design a clinical candidate in under 18 months.
- The number of AI-first drug companies has grown rapidly, with over 100 active as of 2023.
