# Adopting AI that Serves the Needs and Values of Universities

**Full title:** Adopting AI that serves the needs and values of universities -- Final report of the EUA Task-and-Finish Group on Artificial Intelligence

**Authors:** Thomas E. Jorgensen and Clare Phelan

**Publisher:** European University Association (EUA)

**Date:** January 2026

**Pages:** 27

## Purpose

This report distils the outcomes of a two-year EUA work programme on artificial intelligence in higher education. It is intended as a resource for university leaders and representatives overseeing institutional change, offering a starting point for reflection on how to approach AI mindfully. It does not provide a roadmap or set of recommendations but rather serves as inspiration for universities to explore where AI adds value and where it does not.

## Overarching Message

Institutions are best guided by their core missions when laying foundations for AI integration. By respecting the values upheld within their missions, universities can better determine where AI can improve people's everyday experiences of work and learning. Discovering the real value of technologies should be a participative endeavour, undertaken with a spirit of care and enquiry, enabling the whole university community to play an active, conscious role in adopting AI responsibly.
