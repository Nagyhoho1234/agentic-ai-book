# Agentic AI: Theories and Practices

**Editor:** Ken Huang
**Publisher:** Springer Nature Switzerland AG, 2025
**Series:** Progress in IS
**ISBN:** 978-3-031-90026-6

## Book Overview

This edited volume provides a comprehensive examination of AI agents (agentic AI) -- from foundational theory and architecture through domain-specific applications to safety and governance. It covers the full spectrum from single agents to multi-agent systems, from software-only agents to embodied robotic agents, and from technical implementation details to economic and ethical implications.

---

## Chapter Index

### Part I: Foundations and Architecture (Chapters 1-3)

**[Chapter 1: The Genesis and Evolution of AI Agents](ch01_genesis_and_evolution_of_ai_agents.md)**
Defines AI agents through ten key characteristics, traces their history from the 1956 Dartmouth Conference to the present, presents an eight-category taxonomy, identifies five technological drivers of the current renaissance, and showcases OpenAI Operator and Stanford STaR.

**[Chapter 2: AI Agent Tools and Frameworks](ch02_ai_agent_tools_and_frameworks.md)**
Introduces the Seven-Layer AI Agent Architecture (foundation models through ecosystem), compares four major frameworks (AutoGen, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, AutoGPT), contrasts RAG vs. Agentic RAG, and identifies eight key deployment challenges.

**[Chapter 3: Multi-Agent Coordination](ch03_multi_agent_coordination.md)**
The most extensive chapter covering multi-agent systems: coordination techniques (negotiation, cooperation, competition), communication protocols (FIPA-ACL, KQML), conflict resolution, system design, an 11-level capability framework, API design guidance, and real-world applications in smart cities, supply chains, and disaster response.

### Part II: Economics and Business (Chapters 4-5)

**[Chapter 4: The AI Agent Economy](ch04_the_ai_agent_economy.md)**
Explores how AI agents challenge six areas of economic theory, analyzes OpenAI's Economic Blueprint, examines DeepSeek R1's impact on democratizing AI, and extensively covers the decentralized agentic economy enabled by blockchain, tokens, and DAOs with case studies of Virtuals, AI16z, and Terminal of Truth.

**[Chapter 5: AI Agents and Business Workflow](ch05_ai_agents_and_business_workflow.md)**
Covers the transition from RPA to GenAI agents, eight dimensions of workflow transformation, seven areas of full automation, four human-AI collaboration frameworks (Assistant to Autonomous Organization), seven irreplaceable human skills, and real-world case studies from startups (Olive, 11xAI, Ema) and tech giants (Microsoft, Google, Salesforce, Amazon).

### Part III: Security (Chapters 6-7)

**[Chapter 6: AI Agents in Offensive Security](ch06_ai_agents_in_offensive_security.md)**
Examines AI agents in red team operations (Meta GOAT, Google AART, OpenAI RL-based, Microsoft PyRIT), AI-driven social engineering (deepfakes, phishing, voice cloning), software supply chain attacks, bug bounty automation, and vulnerability/zero-day discovery (Google Big Sleep, DeepFuzz design).

**[Chapter 7: AI Agents in Defensive Security](ch07_ai_agents_in_defensive_security.md)**
Covers seven core defensive functions (threat detection through application security), the MELT observability framework, deployment architectures, case studies (Dropzone AI, Darktrace, Microsoft Security Copilot, Seven AI), training environments (CybORG, CAGE, CyGIL), and future trends (adaptive honeypots, meta-learning, human-AI SOC collaboration).

### Part IV: Domain Applications (Chapters 8-11)

**[Chapter 8: AI Agents in Banking](ch08_ai_agents_in_banking.md)**
Comprehensive treatment of AI agents across eight banking applications (credit risk, fraud detection, customer service, personalized banking, risk management, trading, payments, compliance), the "digital worker" concept, fourteen challenges, and an eleven-step preparation roadmap. Key case studies: JP Morgan COiN, Klarna AI assistant, Bank of America Erica, HSBC AML AI.

**[Chapter 9: AI Agents in Insurance](ch09_ai_agents_in_insurance.md)**
Applies the Seven-Layer Architecture to insurance, covers AI-enhanced risk assessment (wearables, IoT, geospatial data, knowledge graphs), automated claims processing (FNOL to payment), fraud detection, customer engagement, and responsible AI governance. Case studies: John Hancock Vitality, Progressive Snapshot, Lemonade, Tractable, Shift Technology.

**[Chapter 10: AI Agents in Healthcare](ch10_ai_agents_in_healthcare.md)**
Examines clinical decision support (multi-agent sepsis management, TrialGPT for trial recruitment), diagnostics (Google Health breast cancer, predictive analytics), drug discovery (AlphaFold 3, CRISPR-GPT), administrative workflows (cobots), patient engagement, preventive healthcare, and future directions (wearables/IoT, robotic surgery, genomics, mental health, global health).

**[Chapter 11: AI Agents in Robotics](ch11_ai_agents_in_robotics.md)**
The second-longest chapter covering the agentic framework for robotics (perception, interaction, reasoning modules), humanoid robot modules, simulation and training (RL, imitation learning, NVIDIA Isaac Sim), spatial intelligence (SLAM, Fei-Fei Li's Large World Models), applications (healthcare, disaster response, underwater), competitive landscape (15 companies), deep dives into NVIDIA/DeepMind/Unitree strategies, governance, workforce impact, and future outlook.

### Part V: Safety and Governance (Chapter 12)

**[Chapter 12: AI Agent Safety and Security Considerations](ch12_ai_agent_safety_and_security.md)**
The capstone chapter covering vulnerabilities (accidental failures and deliberate attacks including adversarial attacks, data poisoning, model theft, VLM pop-up attacks), OWASP Top 10 for AI Agents, goal alignment (alignment problem, motivation drift, representation drift), inter-agent communication security, authentication and identity management (distributed PKI, blockchain identity, behavior-based auth), securing embodied AI agents, and comprehensive agentic AI governance framework.

## Cross-Cutting Themes

1. **Seven-Layer Architecture** (introduced Ch. 2) is applied across domain chapters
2. **Security as a pervasive concern** -- not just Chs. 6-7 and 12, but embedded in every domain chapter
3. **Human-AI collaboration spectrum** -- from assistant to autonomous organization (Ch. 5), reflected in all domain chapters
4. **Multi-agent systems** as the dominant paradigm for complex real-world applications
5. **Responsible AI** -- ethics, fairness, transparency, explainability woven throughout
6. **Practical code examples** provided in most chapters for immediate implementation reference
