# Adopting AI Responsibly: Guidelines for Procurement of AI Solutions by the Private Sector

**Source:** World Economic Forum, in collaboration with GEP | Insight Report, June 2023

## Overview

This report provides a structured, industry-agnostic framework for the responsible procurement of AI solutions by commercial organizations. It was developed by the WEF Centre for the Fourth Industrial Revolution in association with GEP and over 25 community partners from industries including oil and gas, energy, construction, and consulting. The framework is directional rather than prescriptive, emphasizing ethics and sustainability at the core, surrounded by five pillars of responsible AI acquisition.

## The Five Pillars at a Glance

1. **Business Strategy** -- Does the AI solution align with organizational goals and deliver measurable outcomes?
2. **Commercial Strategy** -- Are costs, ROI, and commercial terms structured for long-term value?
3. **Data Strategy** -- Is the data foundation adequate, governed, and ethically managed?
4. **Ethics and Sustainability** -- Does the solution meet fairness, bias, inclusivity, and environmental standards?
5. **Governance, Risk and Compliance** -- Are risk management, regulatory, and audit processes in place?

## Key Takeaway for University Leaders

This framework can be directly adapted for higher education institutions procuring AI tools for administration, research support, student services, or learning analytics. The five-pillar approach provides a ready-made evaluation checklist that procurement offices, IT leadership, and academic governance committees can use to vet AI vendors systematically.
