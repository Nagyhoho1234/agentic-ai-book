# Foreword and Executive Summary

## Summary

The foreword is authored by **Mudit Kumar** (Vice-President, Consulting, GEP) and **Cathy Li** (Head, AI, Data and Metaverse; Member of the Executive Committee, World Economic Forum). They frame the report as a response to the rapid growth of the global AI market -- valued at $136 billion in 2022 with a projected CAGR of 37.3% through 2030 -- and the corresponding lack of standard benchmarks and assessment criteria for responsible AI procurement.

The executive summary positions the report as a practical, industry-agnostic toolkit that helps commercial organizations identify, select, and implement AI solutions responsibly and ethically. It is directional rather than prescriptive, and problem-agnostic rather than problem-specific.

## Key Procurement Guidelines

- AI solutions must be closely aligned with business goals, ethical standards, and regulatory requirements
- Stakeholder collaboration and an enterprise-wide evaluation process are essential
- The framework highlights five key considerations: business strategy, commercial strategy, data strategy, ethics and sustainability, and governance/risk/compliance
- Procurement should serve as the orchestrator driving the implementation of this framework

## Practical Takeaways for University Leaders

- **No standard playbook exists** for AI procurement -- universities are not behind; everyone is building processes from scratch
- The report validates the need for structured evaluation frameworks even for smaller-scale AI deployments (e.g., learning management tools, research analytics)
- AI procurement is not solely an IT decision -- it requires input from business/academic units, data teams, ethics committees, legal, and procurement
- The framework can be customized for the education sector; it is not a one-size-fits-all checklist

## Notable Frameworks

- The report broadly covers: assessing ethical standards and regulatory compliance, aligning solutions with business and commercial goals, and evaluating potential impact
- Emphasizes transparency, accountability, and human-centred design from development to implementation
