## Key Themes Across the Book

1. **AI is an umbrella term.** Generative AI, predictive AI, and content moderation AI are fundamentally different technologies with different strengths and failure modes.

2. **The biggest problems are institutional, not technical.** Better algorithms will not fix broken hiring systems, underfunded schools, or privatized public infrastructure.

3. **Demand peer-reviewed evidence.** The AI hype cycle (company makes claims, media amplifies, adoption occurs, independent evaluation reveals failures) repeats across domains.

4. **Accuracy metrics are routinely misleading.** Base rates, metric choice, and benchmark vs. real-world gaps make impressive numbers meaningless without context.

5. **The reproducibility crisis is real.** Leakage, proprietary models, and inadequate reporting make much AI-based science unreliable.

6. **Cognitive biases make everyone vulnerable.** The halo effect, anchoring, anthropomorphism, and quantification bias all sustain AI myths.

7. **The future is not predetermined.** Active civic engagement, regulation, and institutional reform can steer AI toward public benefit rather than corporate extraction.
