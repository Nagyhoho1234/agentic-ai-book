# Brave New Words: How AI Will Revolutionize Education (and Why That's a Good Thing)

**Author:** Salman Khan
**Published:** 2024
**Publisher:** Viking (Penguin Random House)

## Overview

Salman Khan, founder of Khan Academy, presents a deeply optimistic but grounded vision of how generative AI -- particularly large language models like GPT-4 -- will transform education at every level. Drawing on Khan Academy's partnership with OpenAI and the development of the AI tutor "Khanmigo," Khan argues that AI can democratize access to personalized, world-class education for every student on the planet. The book covers tutoring, the humanities, STEM, mental health, parenting, child safety, teaching, global equity, assessment, college admissions, and the future of work.

## Key Themes Across the Book

1. **AI as tutor, not replacement** -- The AI is the teaching assistant; the human teacher remains essential
2. **The 2-sigma problem** -- Benjamin Bloom's finding that 1-on-1 tutoring produces 2 standard deviations of improvement; AI can deliver this at scale
3. **Educated bravery** -- Khan's recurring phrase for the mindset needed to embrace AI thoughtfully rather than fearfully
4. **Khanmigo** -- Khan Academy's GPT-4-powered AI tutor, built with guardrails to support (not replace) learning
5. **Equity and access** -- AI can be the great equalizer, giving every student access to personalized tutoring regardless of income or geography
6. **Socratic method** -- Khanmigo is designed to guide students through questions rather than give direct answers
7. **Transparency and guardrails** -- Responsible AI requires safeguards against hallucination, bias, and misuse
