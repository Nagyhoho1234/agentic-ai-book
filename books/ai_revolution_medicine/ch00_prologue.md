# Prologue

**Author:** Carey Goldberg

## Summary

The Prologue opens with a vivid, personal scenario: a frail, elderly mother named Rosa -- based on co-author Isaac Kohane's real mother -- navigating the healthcare system. Rosa suffers from multiple chronic conditions (heart failure, diabetes, arthritis) and struggles to get timely, coordinated care. The narrative illustrates the fragmented, slow, and sometimes dangerous reality of healthcare for elderly patients.

The chapter then pivots to imagine what Rosa's experience could look like with an AI medical assistant. In this hypothetical, an AI helps Rosa understand her symptoms, coordinates information between her multiple specialists, reminds her about medications, and flags urgent warning signs -- all in her preferred language and at her comprehension level.

Goldberg introduces the three authors and their distinct perspectives: Peter Lee (Microsoft's Corporate VP for Research), Isaac "Zak" Kohane (chair of Harvard Medical School's Department of Biomedical Informatics), and herself (longtime medical and science journalist). She describes how they gained early access to GPT-4 and were stunned by its medical capabilities -- capabilities that far exceeded previous AI systems.

The Prologue establishes the book's central tension: GPT-4 demonstrates remarkable medical reasoning that could transform healthcare, but it also makes dangerous errors (hallucinations, fabricated citations, inconsistent answers) that make unsupervised use perilous. The book aims to document both the promise and the peril, with extensive real-time demonstrations of GPT-4 interactions.

## Key Medical AI Applications

- **Elderly care coordination:** AI as a personal medical assistant for patients with multiple chronic conditions
- **Multilingual patient communication:** AI communicating with patients in their preferred language
- **Medication management:** Tracking and reminding patients about complex medication regimens
- **Symptom triage:** Helping patients assess whether symptoms require urgent attention
- **Specialist coordination:** Synthesizing information across multiple providers

## Practical Takeaways for Scientists

- GPT-4 represents a qualitative leap over previous AI systems in medical reasoning; scientists should evaluate it with the same rigor they apply to new medical tools
- The book uses a demonstration-based methodology: claims about GPT-4 are supported by actual interaction transcripts, not theoretical arguments
- The authors deliberately chose not to fine-tune GPT-4 for medicine; they tested the general-purpose model, making results more broadly applicable
- Early access to frontier AI models can provide significant scientific advantages in understanding their potential applications
