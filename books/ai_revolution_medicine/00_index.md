# The AI Revolution in Medicine: GPT-4 and Beyond -- Index

**Authors:** Peter Lee, Carey Goldberg, Isaac "Zak" Kohane
**Publisher:** Pearson, 2023
**Foreword by:** Lloyd B. Minor (Dean, Stanford School of Medicine)

## Book Overview

This book explores how GPT-4 and large language models (LLMs) are poised to transform medicine and healthcare. Written by a Microsoft research executive, a medical journalist, and a Harvard medical informatics pioneer, it provides extensive demonstrations of GPT-4's capabilities across clinical diagnosis, patient communication, medical education, healthcare administration, scientific research, and regulatory challenges. The book is structured around live interactions with GPT-4, showing both its impressive competencies and its dangerous limitations.

## Key Themes Across the Book

1. **GPT-4 as a medical reasoning engine** -- capable of passing medical licensing exams, generating differential diagnoses, and interpreting complex clinical scenarios
2. **Human-in-the-loop remains essential** -- hallucinations, factual errors, and lack of grounding in physical examination mean physicians must verify AI output
3. **Healthcare administration as low-hanging fruit** -- documentation, billing codes, prior authorization, and patient communication are immediate high-value applications
4. **Equity and access** -- AI could democratize medical knowledge for underserved populations worldwide, but biases in training data threaten to perpetuate disparities
5. **Regulation as an unsolved problem** -- existing FDA frameworks (SaMD) do not fit general-purpose LLMs; new approaches are urgently needed
6. **Acceleration of biomedical research** -- clinical trial design, patient recruitment, literature synthesis, and drug discovery can all be augmented by LLMs
