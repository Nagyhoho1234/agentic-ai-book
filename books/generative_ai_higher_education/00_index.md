**Authors:** Cecilia Ka Yuk Chan and Tom Colloton
**Publisher:** Routledge, 2024
**ISBN:** 9781003459026
**License:** CC-BY-NC-ND

## Book Overview

This book examines the transformative impact of Generative AI (GenAI), particularly ChatGPT, on higher education. It provides frameworks for AI literacy, curriculum design, assessment redesign, and policy development, while also covering the underlying technology and future predictions. The book is aimed at university faculty, administrators, and policymakers navigating the integration of GenAI tools into teaching and learning.

## Key Frameworks in the Book

- **DAILM** -- Dynamic AI Literacy Model (Chapter 2)
- **AI Literacy Framework** -- Competencies for students and faculty (Chapter 2)
- **AI Ecological Education Policy Framework** -- For institutional policy (Chapter 5)
- **AI Assessment Integration Framework** -- For redesigning assessments (Chapter 4)
- **SARPS** -- Six Assessment Redesign Pivotal Strategies (Chapter 4)
- **Bloom's Taxonomy** applied to AI-era assessment (Chapter 4)
- **SOLO Taxonomy** applied to AI-era assessment (Chapter 4)

## Key Themes

1. GenAI is not a passing trend -- it will fundamentally reshape higher education
2. Banning GenAI is counterproductive; institutions must integrate it thoughtfully
3. Assessment must shift from knowledge reproduction to authentic, process-based evaluation
4. AI literacy is a new essential competency for all graduates
5. Institutional policies must balance innovation with academic integrity
6. The technology is evolving rapidly; frameworks must be adaptive
7. Faculty need support and training, not just mandates
