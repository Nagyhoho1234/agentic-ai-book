**Editor:** Manisha Vohra
**Publisher:** Scrivener Publishing LLC, 2023
**ISBN:** Published by John Wiley & Sons

## Overview

This edited volume provides a comprehensive introduction to digital twin technology, covering its origins, architecture, enabling technologies, and applications across diverse sectors including healthcare, infrastructure, oil and gas, pharmaceuticals, smart cities, and manufacturing. The book is structured as 15 chapters contributed by different authors, making it a broad survey of the field as of 2023.

## Thematic Grouping

### Foundations (Chapters 1--4)
Chapters 1--4 establish the theoretical foundations: the history and evolution of digital twins from NASA's Apollo program through Industry 4.0, the conceptual framework (digital twin prototype, instance, environment), the solution architecture with its five-dimensional model, and the critical role of IoT as the data backbone.

### Enabling Technologies (Chapter 5)
Chapter 5 explores the technologies that make digital twins work: machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, computer vision, AR/VR, and human-robot collaboration.

### Domain Applications (Chapters 6--11)
Chapters 6--11 cover specific sectors: construction and infrastructure management, healthcare (personalized medicine, surgical planning), infrastructure monitoring (SHM with sensors), oil and gas (predictive maintenance, drilling optimization), smart cities (traffic, drainage, power grids), and pharmaceuticals (manufacturing process twins, supply chain).

### Cross-Cutting Topics (Chapters 12--15)
Chapters 12--15 survey applications broadly across agriculture, education, automotive, aviation, weather forecasting, product development, supply chain management, and conclude with a balanced assessment of pros and cons.

## Key Recurring Themes

1. **NASA origins (1960s--1970s)** and Michael Grieves' 2002 PLM presentation as the formal birth of digital twin concepts
2. **Industry 4.0** as the primary driver of digital twin adoption in manufacturing
3. **IoT and sensor data** as the essential data pipeline feeding digital twins
4. **Predictive maintenance** as the most consistently cited use case across all domains
5. **Cyber-physical systems (CPS)** as the theoretical framework linking physical and virtual worlds
6. **Real-time monitoring and what-if simulation** as the two core value propositions
7. **Challenges**: data security, interoperability, high implementation cost, need for domain expertise
