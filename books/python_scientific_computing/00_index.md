# Python for Scientific Computing and Artificial Intelligence -- Index

**Author:** Stephen Lynch
**Publisher:** Taylor & Francis / CRC Press
**Year:** 2023

## Book Structure

The book is divided into three sections with 20 content chapters plus an answers chapter.

---

### Section I: Python Programming

| Chapter | Title | Summary |
|---------|-------|---------|
| 1 | [A Beginner's Guide to Python](ch01_a_beginners_guide_to_python.md) | Python basics: IDLE, data types, loops, conditionals, functions, turtle graphics, and fractals. |
| 2 | [NumPy and Matplotlib](ch02_numpy_and_matplotlib.md) | NumPy arrays, tensors, matplotlib plotting, LaTeX rendering, Jupyter notebooks, and Markdown. |
| 3 | [SymPy](ch03_sympy.md) | Symbolic mathematics with SymPy: calculus, differential equations, linear algebra, and interactive plots. |
| 4 | [More Python, SymPy and the Wallis Sieve Fractal](ch04_more_python_sympy.md) | Advanced Python: data structures, statistics, hypothesis testing, Venn diagrams, and the Wallis sieve fractal. |
| 5 | [Applied Mathematics](ch05_applied_mathematics.md) | Sequences, series, applied calculus in physics and engineering, probability, and hypothesis testing. |

### Section II: Python for Scientific Computing

| Chapter | Title | Summary |
|---------|-------|---------|
| 6 | [Dynamical Systems](ch06_dynamical_systems.md) | Discrete and continuous dynamical systems, population models, bifurcation diagrams, and chaos. |
| 7 | [Chemical Kinetics and SIR Models](ch07_chemical_kinetics.md) | Chemical reaction equations, equilibrium, ozone production, and Pandas DataFrames. |
| 8 | [Sci-Kit Learn and Decision Trees](ch08_scikit_learn.md) | Machine learning with scikit-learn: k-means clustering, decision trees, linear programming, and economics models. |
| 9 | [Finance](ch09_finance.md) | Modern Portfolio Theory, Black-Scholes model, the Greeks, and financial data analysis. |
| 10 | [Chaos and Fractals in Python](ch10_chaos_and_fractals.md) | Nonlinear oscillators, Chua circuit, coupled oscillators, iterated function systems, Mandelbrot set, and multifractals. |
| 11 | [Image Processing](ch11_image_processing.md) | OpenCV and scikit-image for image manipulation, segmentation, brain tumor analysis, and microbe counting. |
| 12 | [Numerical Methods for ODEs](ch12_numerical_methods_odes.md) | Euler method, Runge-Kutta, Adams-Bashforth, vectorized code, and wave equation simulation. |
| 13 | [Numerical Methods for PDEs and Signal Processing](ch13_numerical_methods_pdes.md) | Finite differences for PDEs, heat diffusion, advection, Fourier transforms, and signal processing. |
| 14 | [Nonlinear Dynamics and Hysteresis](ch14_nonlinear_dynamics.md) | Hysteresis, pinched hysteresis, Duffing oscillator, Ikeda map, Josephson junctions, and Newton's equations. |
| 15 | [Statistics](ch15_statistics.md) | Linear regression, Markov chains, statistical tests, Monte Carlo simulation, and probability distributions. |

### Section III: Artificial Intelligence

| Chapter | Title | Summary |
|---------|-------|---------|
| 16 | [Brain Inspired Computing](ch16_brain_inspired_computing.md) | Hodgkin-Huxley model, FitzHugh-Nagumo equations, binary oscillator half-adder, SR flip-flop, and neuromorphic computing. |
| 17 | [Neural Networks and Neurodynamics](ch17_neural_networks.md) | ANN fundamentals, backpropagation, logic gates with neural networks, machine learning on Boston housing data, and stability analysis of neuromodules. |
| 18 | [TensorFlow and Keras](ch18_tensorflow_keras.md) | TensorFlow/Keras introduction, linear regression, XOR gate, Boston housing with deep learning, activation functions, and overfitting. |
| 19 | [Recurrent Neural Networks](ch19_recurrent_neural_networks.md) | Discrete and continuous Hopfield networks, Lyapunov functions, LSTM for predicting chaotic and financial time series. |
| 20 | [Convolutional Neural Networks, TensorBoard and Further Reading](ch20_convolutional_neural_networks.md) | CNNs, convolution and pooling, MNIST classification, TensorBoard, plus overviews of cyber security, ethics in AI, IoT, NLP, and reinforcement learning. |
| 21 | [Answers and Hints to Exercises](ch21_answers_and_hints.md) | Solutions and hints for all chapter exercises with links to GitHub solutions. |
