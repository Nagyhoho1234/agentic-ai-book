# Teaching with AI: A Practical Guide to a New Era of Human Learning

**Authors:** Jose Antonio Bowen and C. Edward Watson
**Publisher:** Johns Hopkins University Press, 2024

## Book Structure

The book is organized into three parts with 12 chapters, an Introduction, and an Epilogue.

### Part I: AI and the New Landscape

- [Chapter 1: What Is AI?](ch01_what_is_ai.md) -- Foundations of artificial intelligence, how LLMs work, and key AI concepts for educators
- [Chapter 2: AI Is Changing Everything](ch02_ai_is_changing_everything.md) -- How AI is transforming work, professions, and the skills students need
- [Chapter 3: Using AI](ch03_using_ai.md) -- Practical guidance on prompting, choosing AI tools, and getting better results
- [Chapter 4: Humanizing AI](ch04_humanizing_ai.md) -- AI's surprising emotional capabilities, empathy, creativity, and Vygotskian collaboration
- [Chapter 5: AI Bias, Risk, and Ethics](ch05_ai_bias_risk_and_ethics.md) -- Bias in training data, hallucinations, privacy concerns, and ethical frameworks

### Part II: Teaching in the Age of AI

- [Chapter 6: Cheating](ch06_cheating.md) -- The history and psychology of academic dishonesty, and how AI changes the equation
- [Chapter 7: AI Policies](ch07_ai_policies.md) -- Developing institutional and course-level AI policies, from banning to embracing
- [Chapter 8: Grading and (Re-)Defining Quality](ch08_grading_and_redefining_quality.md) -- AI-proof rubrics, raising standards, and redefining what constitutes quality work
- [Chapter 9: Feedback and Roleplaying with AI](ch09_feedback_and_roleplaying_with_ai.md) -- Using AI for feedback, tutoring, role-playing, and student engagement

### Part III: Learning with AI

- [Chapter 10: Designing Assignments and Assessments for Human Effort](ch10_designing_assignments_for_human_effort.md) -- Motivation science, assignment design, and process-focused pedagogy
- [Chapter 11: Writing and AI](ch11_writing_and_ai.md) -- Rethinking writing instruction, cognitive artifacts, and AI-resistant writing assignments
- [Chapter 12: AI Assignments and Assessments](ch12_ai_assignments_and_assessments.md) -- A comprehensive catalog of AI-integrated assignment ideas across disciplines

### [Epilogue](ch13_epilogue.md) -- The future of higher education with AI

## Key Themes (from the authors)

- AI is going to change working and thinking
- AI is a new baseline for average or adequate
- AI is a tool that can elevate the creativity and productivity of everyone
- AI is only going to get better and more ubiquitous; specialized AI tools are about to proliferate
- Faculty need to adapt with new policies, assignments, and assessments; AI detection will not be enough nor entirely dependable
- All students will need AI literacy and will need to be able to use AI as a partner and collaborator
