# Chapter 10: Design of Public Surveys

## Summary

This chapter demonstrates ChatGPT's ability to design comprehensive survey questionnaires, refine their scope, and develop full survey-based risk assessment studies. The authors tested GPT-4 on creating questionnaires related to consumer behavior during COVID-19, with impressive results.

## Subsections

### 10.1 Creating a Survey Questionnaire from Scratch

The authors asked GPT-4 to design a 30-question survey on whether mask-wearing during COVID-19 changed cosmetic product use among U.S. consumers (Table 28).

**Results:**
- The model generated a well-structured questionnaire with logical sections: Demographics, Mask-Wearing Behavior, Pre-COVID Product Usage, Post-COVID Product Usage, Product Choices and Preferences, Changes in Application Patterns, and General Questions.
- Section headings, bold/italic fonts, and clear formatting were applied automatically.
- The model went beyond the basic request by adding "general questions" at the end covering broader attitudes and perceptions.
- The questionnaire showed systematic, intelligent design comparable to work by human specialists.

### 10.2 Refining Scopes

The authors then narrowed the survey focus to: (1) adult females aged 18-60, (2) face powders and powdery cosmetics, and (3) added a 10-question mini-questionnaire for those with chronic respiratory diseases (Table 29).

**Results:**
- GPT-4 generated a refined 40-question questionnaire in 8 sections focused on the narrower topic.
- The mini-questionnaire for respiratory disease patients was thoughtful and medically relevant.
- The refined questionnaire provided a solid foundation for an actual survey study.

### 10.3 Designing a Survey-Based Risk Assessment Study

Building on the questionnaire, the authors asked GPT-4 to design a full experimental risk assessment study on inhaling fine particulate matter from powdery cosmetics while wearing face masks (Table 30).

**Results:**
- The model provided a comprehensive study design with: Objectives, Preliminary Steps, Materials and Reagents, Apparatus and Instruments, Experimental Design, QA/QC, Interpretation and Reporting, and Limitations.
- The QA/QC section was particularly strong, covering instrument calibration, blanks, replicates, spike recovery, interlaboratory comparison, documentation, and SOPs.
- When the user challenged aspects of the experimental design (e.g., questioning the need for an inhalation chamber), the model revised its approach, demonstrating adaptive reasoning.
- The model provided detailed descriptions of key equipment (breathing simulator, particle trap) and their connections.

## Practical Takeaways

- ChatGPT can generate detailed, well-structured survey questionnaires with minimal guidance.
- Use iterative refinement to narrow the focus and add specialized sub-questionnaires.
- The model can extend from questionnaire design to full risk assessment study design.
- AI-designed surveys can serve as strong starting points, saving significant time.
- The model's ability to tailor follow-up questions in real-time enables interactive dialogue with survey respondents.
- Environmental scientists can use these tools for tracking consumer behavior changes, gauging community attitudes, and assessing environmental policy impact.
