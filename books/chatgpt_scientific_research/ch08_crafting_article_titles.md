# Chapter 8: Crafting Article Titles

## Summary

This short chapter demonstrates ChatGPT's ability to generate creative, informative, and impactful article titles for research manuscripts. The authors provided the ChatGPT-enabled new Bing with a manuscript outline in PDF format and asked it to suggest titles.

## Key Concepts

- Good titles capture the essence of research and motivate reader engagement.
- Examples of impactful titles in environmental/public health writing: "Silent Spring" (Rachel Carson), "COVID-19 Trashed the Recycling Dream" (Joe Brock), "Spillover" (David Quammen).
- Crafting a concise, informative, and impactful title is traditionally a challenging and time-consuming task.

## Test Results

The model was asked to generate 10 titles for a paper on the environmental impacts of cryptocurrency mining (Table 23). Results:
- The majority of suggested titles were content-relevant and plausible-sounding.
- The authors quickly reached consensus on adopting one title with minor editing: "The environmental paradox of cryptocurrency: How a decentralized technology contributes to centralized energy consumption and pollution."
- When asked for "more lively" titles, the model generated another 10 options, though some veered too far from the outline.
- Even when the user does not adopt any title directly, the model's suggestions provide inspiration and usable phrases.

## Practical Takeaways

- Convert your manuscript outline to PDF and feed it to new Bing for title suggestions.
- Ask for multiple rounds of titles with different styles (formal, creative, lively).
- Use the model's output as raw material -- adopt phrases and structures even if you don't use a title verbatim.
- The process that traditionally takes considerable deliberation can be accelerated to seconds.
