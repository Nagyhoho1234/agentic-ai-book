## 1. Research Integrity and AI Misuse

### Must-Read
**"AI Snake Oil: What AI Can Do, What It Can't, and How to Tell the Difference"**
- Authors: Arvind Narayanan, Sayash Kapoor (Princeton)
- Publisher: Princeton University Press, 2024 (360 pp)
- Why: Named by *Nature* as one of the "10 essential reads of the year." Directly debunks flawed AI science, covers overhyped claims, and teaches how to distinguish real from fake AI capabilities. The closest book to what we need for Ch 16.
- [Princeton UP](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691249131/ai-snake-oil) | [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Snake-Oil-Artificial-Intelligence-Difference/dp/069124913X)

**"Science Fictions: How Fraud, Bias, Negligence, and Hype Undermine the Search for Truth"**
- Author: Stuart Ritchie
- Publisher: Metropolitan Books, 2020 (paperback reissue 2023)
- Why: Foundational text on the replication crisis, p-hacking, fraud. Not AI-specific but essential context. Our book should build on this by showing how AI both worsens and can help detect these problems.
- [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Science-Fictions-Fraud-Negligence-Undermine/dp/1250222699)

### Important References
**"OECD: Artificial Intelligence in Science — Challenges, Opportunities and the Future of Research"**
- Publisher: OECD Publishing, 2023
- Why: Authoritative policy perspective on AI risks in science, including integrity, governance, and institutional recommendations. Free/open access.
- [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/publications/artificial-intelligence-in-science-a8d820bd-en.htm)

### Key Papers (not books, but essential)
- "AI for Scientific Integrity: Detecting Ethical Breaches, Errors, and Misconduct in Manuscripts" — Frontiers in AI, 2025. [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/artificial-intelligence/articles/10.3389/frai.2025.1644098/full)
- "Research Integrity in the Era of AI: Challenges and Responses" — PMC, 2024. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11224801/)
- Sharon Kabel's "Bibliography of GenAI-Fueled Research Fraud from 2025" — comprehensive tracker. [sharonkabel.com](http://sharonkabel.com/genai-fraud/)
- Stat: In 2024, ~13.5% of all PubMed-indexed papers (~200,000 articles) showed LLM processing signatures.

## 2. AI in University Teaching and Supervision

### Must-Read
**"Teaching with AI: A Practical Guide to a New Era of Human Learning" (2nd edition)**
- Authors: José Antonio Bowen, C. Edward Watson
- Publisher: Johns Hopkins University Press, 2024 (2nd ed. updated with reasoning models and agents)
- Why: THE reference for university faculty who teach. Covers course design, assessment in the AI age, the "homework apocalypse," assignment ideas, prompt strategies. Second edition adds agent-based tools.
- [Hopkins Press](https://www.press.jhu.edu/books/title/54122/teaching-ai) | [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Teaching-AI-Practical-Guide-Learning/dp/1421449226)

**"Co-Intelligence: Living and Working with AI"** (we already have this)
- Author: Ethan Mollick
- Ch 7 (AI as Tutor) and Ch 8 (AI as Coach) are directly relevant.

### Important References
**"Teaching and Generative AI: Pedagogical Possibilities and Productive Tensions"**
- Editors: B. Buyserie, T.N. Thurston
- Publisher: Utah State University (open-access), 2024
- Why: Peer-reviewed, multi-disciplinary, focused on higher education teaching practice. Free.
- [USU Open Access](https://www.usu.edu/empowerteaching/publications/books/teaching-ai/)

**"Infinite Education: The Four-Step Strategy for Leading Change in the Age of AI"**
- Author: Dan Fitzpatrick
- Publisher: 2025
- Why: Institutional change perspective on education transformation.

**"AI for Education: Learning in the Age of Artificial Intelligence"**
- Author: Priten Shah
- Publisher: Jossey-Bass/Wiley, 2024
- Why: Adaptive learning systems, automated assessment, AI tutoring, educational data mining.

**"Brave New Words: How AI Will Revolutionize Education"**
- Author: Salman Khan (Khan Academy founder)
- Publisher: Viking/Penguin, 2024
- Why: The AI-as-Socratic-tutor vision (Khanmigo). Endorsed by Bill Gates, Sam Altman. Shows what personalized AI mentoring looks like at scale.
- [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Brave-New-Words-Revolutionize-Education/dp/0593656954)

**"Generative AI in Higher Education: The ChatGPT Effect"** (Open Access)
- Editors: Cecilia Ka Yuk Chan, Tom Colloton
- Publisher: Routledge, 2024 (CC-BY-NC-ND, free)
- Why: Introduces the SARPS framework (Six Assessment Redesign Pivotal Strategies) and AI Assessment Integration Framework. Directly addresses the "homework apocalypse."
- [Routledge](https://www.routledge.com/Generative-AI-in-Higher-Education-The-ChatGPT-Effect/Chan-Colloton/p/book/9781032599045) | [Open Access](https://library.oapen.org/handle/20.500.12657/88443)

**"Second Handbook of Academic Integrity"**
- Editor: Sarah Elaine Eaton
- Publisher: Springer, 2024 (112 chapters)
- Why: Introduces "postplagiarism" concept — hybrid human-AI writing will be the norm; trying to determine where human ends and AI begins is futile. Groundbreaking framework.
- [Springer](https://link.springer.com/referencework/10.1007/978-3-031-54144-5)

**"Teaching and Generative AI: Pedagogical Possibilities and Productive Tensions"** (Open Access)
- Editors: Beth Buyserie, Travis N. Thurston
- Publisher: Utah State University, 2024 (35 chapters, CC-BY-NC-ND)
- Why: 35 interdisciplinary chapters, free. Excellent for collecting practical examples from diverse fields.
- [USU Open Access](https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/teachingai/)

### Key Resources
- UNESCO AI in Education framework — [UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/digital-education/artificial-intelligence)
- Anthropic's "Claude for Education" documentation (Northeastern case study)
- Ethan Mollick's "The Homework Apocalypse" essay series — [One Useful Thing](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/the-homework-apocalypse)

## 3. Change Management and AI Adoption in Universities

### Must-Read
**"Adopting AI That Serves the Needs and Values of Universities"**
- Publisher: European University Association (EUA), 2024/2025
- Why: Directly addresses university AI adoption from a European perspective. Covers governance, ethics, faculty concerns, procurement, and institutional strategy. Perfect for Ch 15.
- [EUA Report](https://eua.eu/publications/reports/adopting-ai-that-serves-the-needs-and-values-of-universities.html)

### Important References
**"Designed for Digital: How to Architect Your Business for Sustained Success"**
- Authors: Jeanne Ross, Cynthia Beath, Martin Mocker (MIT CISR)
- Publisher: MIT Press, 2019 (still the standard framework)
- Why: The operational backbone + digital platform framework applies to universities redesigning around AI.

**"Reset" (Amazon Best Books of 2025)**
- Why: Reads like a six-sigma process change guide. Applicable to institutional AI adoption.

**Stanford Digital Transformation & AI Playbook**
- Publisher: Stanford GSB Executive Education
- Why: Framework for organizational AI transformation, adaptable to university context.
- [Stanford GSB](https://em-execed.stanford.edu/digital-transformation-ai-playbook)

**"Governing the Machine: How to Navigate the Risks of AI and Unlock Its True Potential"**
- Authors: Ray Eitel-Porter, Dr. Paul Dongha, Miriam Vogel
- Publisher: Bloomsbury Business, October 2025
- Why: Practical roadmap for AI governance. Nine core risk areas. More accessible than the Oxford Handbook. University administrators could adapt the frameworks directly.
- [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Governing-Machine-navigate-unlock-potential/dp/139942629X)

**"The Oxford Handbook of AI Governance"**
- Editors: Bullock, Chen, Himmelreich, Hudson, Korinek, Young, Zhang
- Publisher: Oxford University Press, 2024 (1,096 pages)
- Why: THE authoritative reference on AI governance. Heavy but essential for grounding governance discussions.
- [Oxford Academic](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/41989)

### Key Reports (not books, but essential for procurement/policy)
- **EDUCAUSE/ACE: AI Procurement in Higher Education** (2025) — practical procurement checklists. [EDUCAUSE](https://er.educause.edu/articles/2025/3/ai-procurement-in-higher-education-benefits-and-risks-of-emerging-tools)
- **EU Model Contractual Clauses for AI Procurement (MCC-AI)** (March 2025) — legally binding for EU institutions including Debrecen. [IAPP Guide](https://iapp.org/news/a/eu-model-contractual-clauses-for-ai-procurement-a-practical-guide)
- **HEAT-AI Framework** (Frontiers in Education, 2025) — risk-based AI classification for higher education, inspired by EU AI Act. [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2025.1505370/full)
- **Polish academics GenAI adoption study** (Springer, 2025) — Rogers' Diffusion of Innovations applied to 640 academics at 10 Polish universities. Central European context similar to Debrecen. [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10639-025-13835-8)

### Free AI Literacy Resource
**"Fostering AI Literacy: A Guide for Educators in Higher Education"**
- Authors: Fang Yi, Jess Taggart, Bethany Mickel (University of Virginia)
- Publisher: UVA Pressbooks, 2025 (Creative Commons BY-NC-SA)
- Why: Free OER with interactive content. Dedicated chapter on "Critical Evaluation of AI Outputs." Directly usable at Debrecen.
- [Full text free](https://pressbooks.library.virginia.edu/ai-literacy/)

### Key Statistics
- BCG 2024: **70% of AI adoption challenges are people/process problems**, not technical
- EDUCAUSE 2024: **75% of HE institutions lack comprehensive digital strategies**; 55% cite data security as top AI concern
- Polish study 2025: Rogers' diffusion model quantifies what drives and blocks faculty adoption in Central Europe

## 4. AI Evidence Quality and Critical Thinking

### Must-Read
**"AI Snake Oil"** (listed above) — also the best resource for evidence quality assessment.

### Key Papers (no single book dominates this space yet)
- Microsoft Research (Lee et al., 2025): "The Impact of Generative AI on Critical Thinking" — survey of knowledge workers showing AI reduces perceived cognitive effort while encouraging over-reliance. [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/lee_2025_ai_critical_thinking_survey.pdf)
- "Critical Thinking in the Age of AI" — systematic review of 640 documents (ERIC, 2025). [ERIC](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1459623.pdf)
- CHI 2025 paper on "self-reported reductions in cognitive effort" when using GenAI. [ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3713778)

### Key Insight for Our Book
The opacity of AI outputs prevents users from inspecting premises, evidence, or inferential steps. GenAI produces fluent, logical-sounding outputs that may contain omissions, hallucinations, and overconfident claims. **This is the core problem our Ch 2.5 verification section must address.**

## 5. Discipline-Specific AI Guides

### Life Sciences / Biology
**"The Age of AI in the Life Sciences: Benefits and Biosecurity Considerations"**
- Publisher: National Academies Press, 2025
- Why: Comprehensive, authoritative. Covers biology, medicine, and agriculture AI applications.
- [NCBI Bookshelf](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK614606/)

**"Deep Learning for the Life Sciences"**
- Authors: Bharath Ramsundar, Peter Eastman, Patrick Walters, Vijay Pande
- Publisher: O'Reilly, 2019 (updated editions through 2024)
- Why: Practical guide using DeepChem for genomics, microscopy, drug discovery.

### Medicine / Clinical Research
**"The AI Revolution in Medicine: GPT-4 and Beyond"**
- Authors: Peter Lee (Microsoft Research VP), Carey Goldberg, Isaac Kohane (Harvard)
- Publisher: Pearson, 2023 (282 pp)
- Why: Three insiders with early GPT-4 access. Real dialogues annotated with context and risk insights. Covers diagnosis, patient visits, research acceleration.
- [Pearson](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/the-ai-revolution-in-medicine-gpt-4-and-beyond/P200000011399/9780138200138) | [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/AI-Revolution-Medicine-GPT-4-Beyond/dp/0138200130)

**"AI in Clinical Medicine: A Practical Guide for Healthcare Professionals"**
- Editors: Michael Byrne, Xiaoxuan Liu
- Publisher: Wiley-Blackwell, 2023
- Why: Multi-chapter guide covering AI in radiology, pathology, ophthalmology, dermatology, clinical decision support.

### Earth Science / Environment
**"Deep Learning for the Earth Sciences"**
- Editors: Gustau Camps-Valls, Daniel Hahn Tuia, Xiao Xiang Zhu, Markus Reichstein
- Publisher: Wiley, 2021 (paperback reissue 2023)
- Why: Foundational edited volume: CNNs for satellite imagery, RNNs for climate time series, physics-aware deep learning.
- [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Deep+Learning+for+the+Earth+Sciences-p-9781119646143)

### Agriculture
**"Artificial Intelligence in Agriculture"**
- Editors: Rajendra P. Sishodia, Sudhir Kumar Sharma
- Publisher: Academic Press/Elsevier, 2024
- Why: Precision agriculture, crop disease detection, yield prediction, soil monitoring, livestock, robotics. Relevant to Debrecen's Faculty of Agriculture.

### Engineering / Physical Sciences
**"Artificial Intelligence for Science"**
- Publisher: World Scientific, 2024
- Why: Cross-disciplinary: AI for materials discovery, physics simulation, chemical engineering. Includes chapters on agriculture, pathology, and materials.
- [World Scientific](https://www.worldscientific.com/worldscibooks/10.1142/13123)

**"Scientific Machine Learning" (MIT Open Textbook)**
- Author: Christopher Rackauckas et al.
- Publisher: MIT, open access, continuously updated 2023-2025
- Why: PINNs, neural ODEs/PDEs, surrogate modeling. THE reference for Ch 6 advanced sections.
- [book.sciml.ai](https://book.sciml.ai/)

### Social Sciences
**"AI for Social Science Research"**
- Author: Minhwa Lee
- Publisher: Cambridge University Press, 2024
- Why: NLP for text analysis, computational social science, sentiment analysis, survey automation, ethics.

**"Computational Analysis of Communication"**
- Authors: van Atteveldt, Trilling, Arcila Calderon
- Publisher: Wiley, 2023
- Why: Python-based NLP/ML for media analysis, political communication, digital humanities.

### Chemistry
**"Applied Machine Learning for Chemists"**
- Authors: Tobias Schwander, Kevin Maik Jablonka
- Publisher: Royal Society of Chemistry, 2024
- Why: Hands-on guide for bench chemists. Molecular property prediction, reaction optimization, spectroscopy.
